§ 01 • Teaching Philosophy

Why parents choose us over big coaching classes.

We do not run an assembly line. Education works when the teacher knows each child by their name, their handwriting, and their weakest chapter.

“If a student doesn’t understand a step in geometry, an app notification won’t help them. A teacher sitting across the table will.”
01

We cap every batch at 15 students so no child gets lost in the crowd.

In large commercial institutes in Kothrud with 50 or 60 students per batch, quiet children sit in the back and nod along. Here, the teacher checks every single notebook before class ends. If a student is stuck on a step, we catch it on the spot.

02

Weekly written tests on Sundays, marked and returned by Wednesday.

Board exams are written with pen on paper, not tapped on a mobile screen. Every second Sunday, students write a full-format test. Every test is step-marked with red pen corrections so students know exactly where they lose marks.

03

Direct phone calls with the teacher, not an admissions counselor.

When parents call with a concern about homework, test scores, or school unit tests, you talk directly to Sachin Sir. No receptionist, no tele-calling executives, and no corporate delays.

§ 06 • Clear Answers

Frequently asked questions.

Straightforward answers for parents evaluating coaching for the upcoming academic year.

What is your fee structure, and are there extra charges for tests or materials? +

Fees range from Rs. 2,400 to Rs. 3,200 per month depending on the student’s class (Class 8 to 10). There are zero hidden charges: all printed chapter revision notes, formula sheets, weekly Sunday test papers, and model answer copies are included in the monthly fee.

Fees are payable on a monthly basis by the 7th of each calendar month. We do not demand whole-year upfront payments.

How strictly do you enforce the 15-student limit per batch? +

Strictly 15 students. Our classroom has exactly 15 wooden study chairs around the main desk. Once the 15th seat is filled, admissions for that batch close immediately and subsequent applicants are placed on our waitlist.

We have refused requests from friends and relatives to add “just one more seat” every single year since 2011.

Can my child attend a free trial or demo class before enrolling? +

Yes. Every prospective student is invited to attend two full trial classes with the running batch. They will solve problems on the board with other students, receive the homework sheet, and get a clear sense of our teaching style.

You only pay fees if both the student and parent feel comfortable continuing after the demo.

What happens if a student misses a class due to illness or school functions? +

We do not use pre-recorded generic video apps. If a student misses a lecture with prior notice, they attend our Saturday evening or Sunday morning doubt clinic where Sachin Sir sits down with them one-on-one to review the missed concepts and numericals.

How often are tests conducted, and how do parents stay informed? +

Full-length chapter tests happen every alternate Sunday from 8:30 AM to 11:30 AM. Tests follow the official board question paper blueprint (CBSE / State Board).

Evaluated answer sheets with red pen markings and step scores are returned directly to the student on Wednesday. Marks and attendance updates are messaged to parents via WhatsApp the same day.
